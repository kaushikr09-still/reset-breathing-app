About Reset

Reset is a free, ad-free breathing app. Choose what you need (to calm down, to focus or to sleep) and follow a guided breathing exercise. No sign-up needed.

Breathing practices like pranayama have deep roots in yoga. Reset's exercises are modern, research-informed techniques built on the same idea: your breath can change how you feel.

Privacy: no account or email is collected. If you use the stress check-in, your ratings are saved against an anonymous ID that is not linked to your name or email. Your streak is stored on your device.

Reset is a relaxation aid, not medical advice.
